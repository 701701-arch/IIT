Before you go

Run the model on your laptop

An Arduino UNO is too small for a trained model. Send sensor readings over USB serial, classify them in Python, and send the verdict back to the LEDs.

Keep motors off the 5V pin

Pumps and motors need their own supply, a shared ground, and a diode across the load. Use the relay or L293D from your kit.

Calibrate before demo day

The pH sensor needs the two buffer powders, and TDS readings drift with temperature. Test every sensor at home first.
